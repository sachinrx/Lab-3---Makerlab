The Best Movie I’ve Seen Lately

The Covenant

The best movie I’ve seen lately is The Covenant. The film is about a US Army soldier and his Afghan interpreter during a conflict in Afghanistan. I enjoyed the movie because it had lots of action, tension, and emotion without feeling fake.

What I Liked

What I liked most about the movie was the relationship between the soldier and the interpreter. The movie portrays their mutual trust and willingness to go above and beyond to help each other, and also the action scenes were ok, along with the plot of the movie.

Why I Recommend It

I would recommend The Covenant since it has a decent plot and good action. It is not only a war film with nonstop fighting. It shows us loyalty, survival, and the impacts of leaving a soldier behind.
